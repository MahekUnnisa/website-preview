Most productivity apps fail for one simple reason: **They ask you to change where you work.**

We have all been there. This is the typical trajectory:

- You find a shiny new productivity tool.
- You spend hours setting up workspaces, dashboards, and tags.
- Two weeks later, you stop opening it.

Why? It is always the **context-switching tax**! In simpler terms, every time you leave your active browser tab, where your emails, documents, research, and work actually happen, to open another app, you break your flow state.

Instead of saving time, you are constantly juggling tabs, copying and pasting data, and managing the tool instead of doing the work.
