A few years back, when we started building DevBytes, our mission was simple but ambitious: **keep busy developers informed without drowning in noise.**

Developers were (and still are) overwhelmed. They juggle endless feeds, newsletters, Reddit threads, Twitter/X, blogs, and GitHub trend.
As a result, they end up wasting hours hunting for signal in the noise.

DevBytes solved that by curating the most relevant tech, AI, coding news, and distilling it into **64-word summaries**. No clutter. No ads. Just high-signal insights you can absorb in 5-7 minutes a day.

What we learned along the way was eye-opening:

- **Lesson #1:** All ambitious devs are productivity junkies.
- **Lesson #2:** Current tools often fall short on their expectations.
- **Lesson #3:** Devs are open to trying new tools.
- **Lesson #4:** Devs haggle, they won't pay unless they are satisfied.

That's exactly why we developed **Zero**, a Chrome Extension.

Zero is the _invisible AI work assistant_ that turns unstructured chaos (tasks scattered across Slack, email, notes, Jira, etc.) into real productivity. It intelligently **pulls, prioritizes, schedules, and manages** work.

I'm thrilled to introduce Zero not just as a new product, but as a true assistant that can make developers significantly more effective.

If you're a developer tired of context-switching and productivity theater, I'd love for you to **try Zero** and share your honest feedback.
