When we started building ZeroAI, we realized something frustrating about the productivity space: Most tools actually **require more work**. They are just another app to check, another list to maintain, and another source of stress.

We wanted to build the exact opposite. Hence, **ZeroAI** was born.

It's an AI assistant that eliminates chaotic to-do lists by automatically turning tasks, chats, emails, and meeting action items into scheduled calendar blocks.

## We named it Zero because our entire philosophy is built on elimination:

- **Zero disconnected to-do lists:** If it's not on your calendar, it doesn't exist.
- **Zero context switching:** Tasks are pulled directly from your chats, tickets, emails, and meetings, then intelligently mapped into real-time blocks.
- **Zero manual busywork:** Write a task in plain English → ZeroAI estimates effort, checks your calendar, suggests optimal slots, and books it with full context.
- **Zero forgotten action items:** Meetings are transcribed in real-time, action items are extracted, and events are automatically booked.
