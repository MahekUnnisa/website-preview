It's not how many lines of code you ship or how many tickets you close in a day.

Real productivity is simple but rare:

- Deep focus on hard problems
- Shipping high-quality work
- Ending the day with clarity, and not exhaustion
- Protecting flow state from the constant barrage of Slack pings, meetings, emails, etc.

Most devs lose hours every week just bouncing between tasks, managing to-do lists, and sitting in meetings that go nowhere. But once you clear out that noise? **That's when the real work happens.**
