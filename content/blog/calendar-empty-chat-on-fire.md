Can you do 2 quick exercises for me?

## First: Look at your calendar.

What do you see? Blank blocks, white space, "free" time.

## Second: Now open Slack, Teams, email, or whatever chat is currently vibrating in your pocket.

What do you see? Requests, follow-ups, quick questions, vague action items from the last meeting, deadlines that somehow never made it out of the thread.

And by 6 pm you are staring at a full day of activity, wondering why the things that actually matter still haven't moved.

The simple law that fixes this:

**If it isn't on the calendar, it doesn't exist.**
