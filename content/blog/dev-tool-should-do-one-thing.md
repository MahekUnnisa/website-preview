In a world full of bloated "all-in-one" platforms that promise everything but deliver notification fatigue and context-switching hell, the best tools stay laser-focused. They solve a sharp, painful problem so cleanly that you wonder how you ever lived without them.

That's exactly the philosophy behind **ZeroAI**.

ZeroAI does one thing brilliantly: It turns the chaos of your work life (Slack threads, emails, meeting notes, scattered tasks) into **protected time on your calendar**.

No more to-do lists that gather digital dust. No more "I'll do it later" that turns into missed deadlines. *If it isn't on the calendar, it doesn't exist*, and ZeroAI makes sure it gets there.

## Here's how it works:

- **Plain English task input:** Type or paste a task (`Review the auth service refactor by Friday`), and ZeroAI intelligently parses it, estimates effort, checks your calendar, and suggests optimal focus blocks. One click and it's scheduled with full context.
- **Meeting transcription & action items:** It joins your video calls, transcribes accurately, extracts decisions, owners, and deadlines, then automatically creates calendar events for your follow-ups.
- **Deep work protection:** It fights context-switching by bridging where work happens (chats, tickets, email) to where work gets done (your calendar).

If you are tired of drowning in async communication while your actual coding time evaporates, ZeroAI is the focused companion you need.
