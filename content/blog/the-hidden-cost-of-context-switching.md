Developers do not lose hours to meetings alone. They lose them to the recovery time between meetings — the Slack reply that pulls you out of flow, the PR review that lands mid-debug, the standup action item nobody wrote down.

ZeroAI treats those fragments as first-class work: capture them, schedule them, and defend the blocks where real coding happens.

## What changes when chaos becomes calendar:

- Commitments from Slack stop living only in threads
- Focus blocks get defended before someone books over them
- End-of-day close-out files unfinished work into tomorrow
