ZeroAI is not yet another productivity tool. It is an **execution layer** that bridges the gap between where work actually happens (Slack/Teams threads, async comments, meetings, PR reviews, emails) and where work gets shipped (your calendar and protected focus blocks).

## Traditional productivity tools (Todoist, Notion, Asana, etc.) create more work:

- You are constantly copy-pasting tasks from chats, standups, and issue comments into yet another app
- Your to-do list lives in a silo, completely disconnected from your real schedule
- They increase context-switching and tool fatigue

## ZeroAI flips the model.

It lives where you already work. Highlight a message, drop a quick note in plain English (`fix auth bottleneck before Friday`), or let it listen to your standup.

ZeroAI automatically:

- Parses the task with proper context
- Estimates effort
- Finds the best open slots on your calendar
- Creates rich calendar events with links back to the original thread/PR/doc

In short: Most tools give you a prettier to-do list. ZeroAI gives you a real **execution system**, a browser-native chief of staff that closes the loop between discussion and delivery.
