Developers are lazily chaotic, and honestly, we should be.

Our brainpower is best spent solving complex engineering problems, architecting clean systems, and shipping code. Yet, most of our daily energy gets drained before writing a single line of code. This is what most days look like:

- A critical decision is made in a Slack thread.
- An edge-case bug gets brought up in a Meet call.
- A PM tags you on a Linear issue.
- An urgent update hits MS Teams at 4:55 PM.

By the end of the day, you have 14 open browser tabs, a half-baked to-do list, and an uneasy feeling that you forgot something important.

Our focus is dying a death by a thousand context-switches.
