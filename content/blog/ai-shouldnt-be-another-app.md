AI was supposed to save us time, but for most people, it just created another tab to manage.

Copying text from Slack, pasting it into ChatGPT, asking for action items, copy-pasting those back into a to-do list, and then trying to figure out when to actually do the work... that isn't productivity. That's just **glorified admin work**.

AI shouldn't be another app you have to manage. **It should live where your work happens.**

## That's the exact philosophy behind ZeroAI.

It sits in your Chrome browser and removes the context-switching tax:

- Instantly pulls action items from emails, chats, and meetings
- Turns those tasks into real time blocks on your calendar
- Lets you summarize, note, and command anything from the tab you're already on
- Includes focus protection with built-in Pomodoro sessions

**Stop managing your AI. Let it remove the friction instead.**
