Devs, be honest. How many open tabs of "tasks" are you currently ignoring? Jira board. Linear. GitHub issues. Slack threads. Notion page. Sticky notes on your monitor. Is my guess right?

You write them down, and feel productive for 30 seconds. Then reality hits: meetings, context switches, "quick" reviews, and the actual deep work.

By Friday the list is longer than it was on Monday.

To-do lists are a lie we tell ourselves. But, do you know what doesn't lie? **Our calendars!**

*If it isn't on the calendar, it doesn't exist.* That is the only rule that actually works for people who ship code.

## And this is where ZeroAI comes in.

It is a lightweight Chrome extension that sits in your browser and turns the chaos (Slack messages, emails, notes, meeting transcripts, issue trackers) into **scheduled calendar blocks**. Write a task in plain English, it estimates duration, finds open slots before the deadline, and protects your focus time. Meetings get transcribed and action items land on your calendar automatically.

It is built for devs who need to guard deep work instead of managing yet another list.
